## 2. 100-Point Media Quality Scoring System
Each photograph and video was evaluated against our 7-factor weighted framework:
- **Visual Sharpness & Clarity (20 Pts):** Resolution, depth of field, lack of motion blur or digital artifacting.
- **Composition & Framing (15 Pts):** Rule of thirds, balance, subject prominence, contextual background.
- **Authenticity & Realism (15 Pts):** Genuine grassroots moments; zero artificial staging or commercial stock aesthetic.
- **Storytelling & Emotional Impact (15 Pts):** Conveys the spirit of *"Not Me But You"*, volunteer grit, community empathy.
- **NSS Relevance & Identity (15 Pts):** Displays NSS badges, banners, t-shirts, Shramdaan activities, or civic duty.
- **Website Suitability (10 Pts):** Aspect ratio adaptability, responsiveness, whole-frame fidelity (`object-fit: contain`).
- **Technical Compression & Performance (10 Pts):** Modern web formats (WebP, optimized MP4), fast load times.

### Classification Tiers:
- **Tier A (90–100 Pts) — Exceptional / Featured:** Primary hero banner, event covers, dignitary spotlights.
- **Tier B (75–89 Pts) — Recommended:** Single event gallery stages, timeline chronicles, video hub cards.
- **Tier C (60–74 Pts) — Secondary Archive:** Filterable day-by-day photo archive.
- **Tier D (< 60 Pts) — Rejected:** Blurry images, duplicates, or unverified individual selfies (strictly excluded).
