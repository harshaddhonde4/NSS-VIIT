## 2. Institutional Nomenclature Standards
- Always refer to the unit as **"Unit A-70, NSS VIIT Pune"** or **"National Service Scheme, VIIT Pune"**.
- Never refer to NSS VIIT as a "company", "firm", or "commercial venture".
- All volunteers are referred to as **"Student Volunteers"** or **"NSS Volunteers"**.
- Faculty coordinators are referred to as **"NSS Programme Officers"** (PO).
